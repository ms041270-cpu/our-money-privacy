# Privacy Policy — Our Money

**Effective date: September 29, 2026**

Our Money is a personal finance application developed by **Michael Sinitski**.

## Data Collection

Our Money does not collect, transmit, or store users' personal or financial data on servers operated by the developer or by third parties.

Financial information entered by the user, including income, expenses, categories, accounts, recurring transactions, amounts, dates, and comments, is stored locally on the user's device.

The application does not require registration or a user account.

Our Money does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or cloud synchronization.

## Local Data Storage

Application data is stored locally on the user's device. The developer does not have access to this data.

The application provides backup and restore functionality. Backup files are created and managed by the user.

The application can also generate Excel and PDF documents from locally stored data.

## Sharing and Email

Our Money allows users to share or send documents using applications installed on their device. These actions occur only when explicitly initiated by the user.

The application may store an email address locally on the device for the user's convenience when preparing an email. This email address is not transmitted to the developer.

Any data or files shared through another application are subject to the privacy practices of the service or application selected by the user.

## Data Security

Because financial and personal data is stored locally, users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device and any backup or exported files they create.

Our Money does not transmit the locally stored financial database to the developer.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data remains on the user's device until it is edited or deleted by the user, the application's local data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Backup, Excel, PDF, or other exported files created by the user remain in locations selected or controlled by the user and can be deleted by the user using the appropriate device or file-management tools.

Since Our Money does not maintain user accounts or store user data on developer-operated servers, there is no server-side user account or associated server-side personal data to delete.

## Children's Privacy

Our Money is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated accordingly.
